Northstar Retail — Client Success Plan

Purpose
The implementation will connect the Northstar product catalogue to the new commerce platform and prepare the support team for launch.

Decisions
The team selected a phased rollout. The pilot will include the Melbourne and Sydney stores before the national release. CSV remains the approved fallback for the first release.

Milestones and commitments
Data mapping must be approved by 18 October 2026. The pilot launch is scheduled for 4 November 2026. Priya owns the mapping review. Jordan will deliver support-team training before 30 October 2026.

Risks and blockers
The client has not confirmed whether archived SKUs should migrate. API rate-limit documentation is still outstanding. Both issues may delay final acceptance if unresolved by the mapping deadline.

Success criteria
The pilot is accepted when catalogue updates sync successfully, support staff complete training, and rollback instructions have been tested.
